Our Diagnostic Services

We offer a comprehensive range of laboratory services to meet your healthcare needs. Our expert team ensures timely and precise results.

Hematology

Our hematology department provides comprehensive analysis of blood and blood-forming tissues to diagnose and manage a wide range of blood disorders. We use advanced automated analyzers to provide accurate and quick results for tests like Complete Blood Count (CBC) and blood smears.

Biochemistry

We measure levels of chemical substances in blood and other body fluids to assess organ function and screen for various conditions. Our tests include liver function tests, kidney function tests, lipid profiles, and blood sugar tests to provide a clear picture of your metabolic health.

Serology

Detecting antibodies and antigens in blood serum to identify infections, autoimmune diseases, and other conditions. We perform tests for a wide range of viral, bacterial, and parasitic diseases. Our state-of-the-art equipment ensures accurate and timely results for conditions like hepatitis, dengue, and HIV.
