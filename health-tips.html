Health Tips

1

Check your blood sugar regularly

Adults over 30 or with a family history of diabetes should get their blood sugar checked from time to time. A fasting sugar test needs about 8 hours without food, while HbA1c shows your average sugar over 2-3 months and needs no fasting.

2

Know your Vitamin D level

Low Vitamin D is common. A simple blood test shows your level. Please do not start high-dose supplements on your own; let your doctor decide the right dose after the test.

3

Watch for thyroid warning signs

Ongoing tiredness, unexplained weight change, hair fall or feeling too cold or hot can be linked to the thyroid. A thyroid profile (TSH, T3, T4) helps your doctor understand what is going on.

4

Prepare properly for a lipid profile

For a cholesterol (lipid) test, doctors usually advise fasting for 9-12 hours. Drink plain water, avoid heavy or oily food the night before, and follow your doctor's instructions.

5

CBC: the everyday health check

A Complete Blood Count (CBC) helps check for anemia, infections and other blood problems. It usually needs no special preparation, but tell us about any medicines you are taking.

6

Before any blood test

Carry your doctor's prescription, drink water unless told otherwise, and ask whether fasting is needed for your test. If you are unsure, message us on WhatsApp before you come.

This information is for general awareness only and is not medical advice. Please consult your doctor about your health.
